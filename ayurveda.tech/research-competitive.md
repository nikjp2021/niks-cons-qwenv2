## 2. Competitor Deep Dives

### 2.1 Planet Ayurveda — planetayurveda.com

**What they do:** Herbal product manufacturer (500+ products), online video consultations, massive knowledge library (diseases A–Z, herbs A–Z), global shipping.

**Visual & Design Analysis:**
- **Colors:** Green-dominant (#2D6A4F-like), gold/amber accents, clinical whites
- **Typography:** Generic system fonts, no distinctive pairing; heavy use of all-caps small caps for headings
- **Layout:** Dense, cluttered homepage with 25+ banner carousel slides; enormous grid of 50+ disease icons
- **Content Strategy:** Quantity-over-quality — thousands of articles but low readability; author-byline with doctor credentials throughout
- **Patterns:** Herb SVG icons for categories, doctor profile cards, testimonial carousel, certification logos footer

**What works:**
- Massive SEO moat from 15,000+ pages of disease content
- Clear authority signals (25 years, 1M+ customers, certifications)
- Multi-channel contact (phone, WhatsApp, chat, email)

**What's broken (opportunities for us):**
- **Information overload** — choice paralysis from the first viewport
- **No brand soul** — generic healthcare feel, no story, no personality
- **No dual-path clarity** — consultation and product shopping are conflated
- **Dated design** — early-2010s WordPress aesthetic undermines trust
- **No pricing transparency** — must register to learn costs

---

### 2.2 Jiva Ayurveda — jiva.com

**What they do:** India's largest Ayurveda clinic chain (80+ clinics, 500+ doctors, 15M+ patients consulted). Online video consultations, product store, Panchakarma therapies, TV shows, hospital chain (Jivagram).

**Visual & Design Analysis:**
- **Colors:** Green primary, orange accents, white backgrounds
- **Typography:** Custom sans-serif for headings (rounded), sans-serif body — clean but generic
- **Layout:** Disease-category card grid, doctor profile grid, testimonial grid, step-by-step process section
- **Content Strategy:** Disease-focused navigation (15+ categories), diagnostic marker content, home remedies in Hindi/English, TV show schedule
- **Patterns:** "Book Free Consultation" sticky CTA, Google reviews carousel, clinic locator map

**What works:**
- Massive trust signals (1.5M+ treated, 80 clinics, 30+ years)
- Strong social proof (video testimonials, celebrity endorsements, 14K+ Google reviews)
- Clear step-by-step consultation process
- Omnichannel presence (clinics, online, TV, store)

**What's broken (opportunities for us):**
- **Design feels corporate** — lacks the warmth and soul Ayurveda should evoke
- **No brand bridge** between ancient and modern — just a standard healthcare site
- **Practitioner path is invisible** — entirely client-focused, no visible consultant value proposition
- **Content overload** — massive nav with 200+ condition pages; overwhelming for new visitors
- **Generic aesthetic** — could be any healthcare brand, not specifically Ayurveda

---

### 2.3 The Ayurveda Experience — theayurvedaexperience.com

**What they do:** Premium D2C Ayurvedic skincare/wellness e-commerce brand. Product lines (iYURA, Ajara, A. Modernica), educational courses, dosha quiz, skincare consultations. US-based, global shipping.

**Visual & Design Analysis:**
- **Colors:** Warm neutrals (beige, sand, stone), deep terracotta, gold accents, black text
- **Typography:** Elegant serif for headings (similar to Playfair Display), clean sans-serif body — editorial feel
- **Layout:** Magazine-style hero, product grid with lifestyle photography, carousel-based discovery, bundle promotions
- **Content Strategy:** Blog-driven education, dosha quiz as lead magnet, "Skin Concierge" personalized recommendations, award badges throughout
- **Patterns:** Sliding carousels for product categories, "Shop by Concern" navigation, sticky cart, Apple Pay / PayPal

**What works:**
- **Premium brand experience** — the most visually refined competitor
- **Strong content-to-commerce funnel** (quiz → article → product)
- **Global audience** — designed for Western consumers, not just India
- **Clean ingredient policy** as differentiator
- **Award badges** (Beauty Shortlist, Pure Beauty Awards) for social proof

**What's broken (opportunities for us):**
- **Skincare-only** — no healthcare consultation platform
- **No practitioner marketplace** — purely product D2C
- **Very US-centric** — pricing in USD, English-only
- **No dual-audience model** — one customer type only
- **Product-focused, not service-focused** — fundamentally different model from ours

### 2.4 Arya Vaidya Sala Kottakkal — aryavaidyasala.com

**What they do:** 120+ year old charitable Ayurvedic institution. Manufacturing, hospitals (inpatient + outpatient), 27 branch clinics, research center, publications, herbal estate, Ayurveda college, museum.

**Visual & Design Analysis:**
- **Colors:** Traditional deep green, cream, gold — very heritage
- **Typography:** Standard web fonts, no distinctive pairing
- **Layout:** Government/institutional feel — header-heavy, dense text, long scrolling with many image banners
- **Content Strategy:** Institutional authority (century of tradition), former presidents as testimonials, publication catalog, research papers
- **Patterns:** Tulsi (holy basil) decorative SVG, thulasi icons, VR 360° tour, app download prompts

**What works:**
- **Ultimate authority** — 120+ years of institutional trust
- **Celebrity testimonials** — former presidents, prime ministers
- **Comprehensive ecosystem** — hospital, college, research, pharmacy, museum
- **Quality signals** — GMP, ISO, US-FDA certifications

**What's broken (opportunities for us):**
- **Severely dated website** — institutional, not consumer-friendly
- **No digital booking flow** — phone/email-only appointment booking
- **No dual-path CTA** — no clear practitioner entry point
- **Content density** — publication catalog on homepage has zero curation
- **No brand personality** — feels like a government portal, not a healing brand

---

### 2.5 Lybrate — lybrate.com (adjacent competitor)

**What they do:** General telehealth platform with Ayurveda as one category. Doctor marketplace for all specialities (not Ayurveda-specific).

**Visual & Design Analysis:**
- **Colors:** Blue primary, white — generic healthcare
- **Typography:** System sans-serif
- **Layout:** Doctor search + filter, listing with ratings, consultation mode toggle (text/audio/video)
- **Patterns:** Filter-by-fee, doctor ratings, "consult privately" value prop

**What works:**
- Marketplace model with clear doctor discovery
- Transparent pricing (fee range filter)
- Multiple consultation modes

**What's broken:**
- **Generic** — no specialization, no brand personality
- **No Ayurveda-specific content or positioning**
- **No platform for practitioners to build practice** — just a listing

## 4. Positioning White Space

**The Opportunity:**  
No competitor currently bridges **ancient Ayurvedic authenticity** with **modern tech-forward digital experience** in a **two-sided marketplace** model.

| Competitor | Authenticity | Modern Design | Two-Sided Marketplace |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planet Ayurveda | ★★★★ | ★ | ★ |
| Jiva Ayurveda | ★★★★ | ★★ | ★ |
| The Ayurveda Experience | ★★★ | ★★★★ | ★ |
| Kottakkal AVS | ★★★★★ | ★ | ★ |
| **Ayurveda.Tech (target)** | **★★★★** | **★★★★★** | **★★★★★** |

**Our white space:**
1. **The only modern, design-forward Ayurveda consultation platform** — competitors look dated
2. **The only true two-sided marketplace** — clients AND practitioners as first-class citizens
3. **The only platform with transparent consultation pricing** — no hidden costs
4. **The only brand that consciously bridges tradition (.tech + serif/sans-serif) in design language**
5. **Content as conversion engine** — dosha education, seasonal guides, practitioner spotlights driving bookings

---

## 5. Key Takeaways for Brand Architecture

1. **Don't compete on authority** (we can't beat 120-year institutions) — **compete on experience, clarity, and modernity**
2. **Dual-path is our superpower** — no competitor serves both audiences equally
3. **Design is our moat** — a polished, premium experience signals trust better than years in business
4. **Knowledge must drive conversion** — educational content about doshas, seasons, and wellness should always lead to a booking CTA
5. **Transparency wins** — show pricing, practitioner credentials, and consultation outcomes upfront
6. **Avoid the "green healthcare" trap** — our amber-brown palette is a deliberate differentiator from the sea of generic green Ayurveda sites
