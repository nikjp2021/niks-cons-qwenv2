## Visual Style Specifications

### Overall Image Treatment
- Warm amber-toned overlay at 5-10% opacity on hero and feature images
- Subtle vignette (darken edges by 10-15%) on section background images
- No heavy Instagram-style filters
- Desaturated green tone for botanical/educational imagery only
- Color temperature: warm (shift towards amber 5500K-6500K equivalent)

### Dosha Color Associations
| Dosha | Primary Color | Secondary Color | Imagery Elements |
|-------|--------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Vata | #C4956A (warm beige/sand) | #F5EDE3 (cream) | Air, movement, wind, dry leaves, flowing fabric |
| Pitta | #E06060 (warm red) | #2D6A4F (forest green) | Fire, sun, water, lotus, red/orange elements |
| Kapha | #40916C (forest green) | #8B5A2B (amber-brown) | Earth, water, flowers, lush growth, dew |

### Practitioner Photography Style
- Natural window light (golden hour quality preferred)
- Warm color temperature
- Authentic expressions (laughing, genuine smiles — not posed corporate headshots)
- Clothing: warm neutrals, cotton fabrics, traditional elements (kurta, saree) where appropriate
- Background context: plants, books, natural textures
- Diverse representation: age, ethnicity, body type

### Texture & Overlay Specifications
- Background noise texture: SVG fractal noise at 3% opacity (defined in tokens.css)
- Warm gradient overlays: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(255,251,245,0.3), rgba(245,237,227,0.5))`
- No pure white backgrounds in images — always warm-tinted
- Image borders: none (no strokes, no frames)

## Technical Specifications

### Format Decision
- **All photographic images:** WebP (best compression/quality ratio for web)
- **Icons and illustrations:** SVG where possible, WebP fallback
- **Favicon:** PNG with SVG fallback
- **OG Image:** JPEG (max quality) or PNG

### Responsive Image Requirements
For hero and large feature images, provide:
| Breakpoint | Width | Crop |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Mobile | 375px | Vertical center crop, tighter framing |
| Tablet | 768px | Standard crop |
| Desktop | 1280px | Full width |
| Large | 1920px | Full width, uncropped |

### Color Profile
- sRGB (standard web color space)
- No CMYK or wide-gamut required
- Embedded ICC profile not necessary

### File Naming Convention
`{section}-{descriptor}-{variant}.webp`
- Examples: `hero-home-desktop.webp`, `dosha-vata-card.webp`, `practitioner-sharma-portrait.webp`, `testimonial-priya-avatar.webp`
