# Brand Voice Guide — Ayurveda.Tech

**Tagline:** "Ancient Wisdom for Modern Wellness"  
**Personality:** grounded, progressive, trusted  
**Tone:** warm-authoritative — the voice of a wise guide

---

## 1. Voice Principles

### Our Voice Is:
| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Warm** | We speak like a trusted advisor, not a textbook. Our tone is inviting, reassuring, and human. |
| **Authoritative** | We know Ayurveda deeply. We use precise terminology (Vata, Pitta, Kapha, dosha, Prakriti) with confidence and clarity — never apologizing for traditional knowledge. |
| **Bridge-building** | We translate ancient wisdom for modern lives. We connect Ayurvedic principles to everyday wellness challenges without dumbing either side down. |
| **Dual-audience aware** | We speak with equal fluency to wellness seekers and Ayurvedic practitioners — each feels seen, understood, and valued. |
| **Empowering** | We don't prescribe; we educate and invite. Our language empowers visitors to make informed choices about their health journey. |

### Our Voice Is NOT:
| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails |
|---|---|
| Cold / clinical | Undermines the warmth of Ayurveda |
| New-age / mystical | Damages credibility with modern audience |
| Jargon-heavy without explanation | Excludes newcomers to Ayurveda |
| Overly salesy | Breaks trust — health decisions require confidence, not pressure |
| Generic wellness | Makes us interchangeable with every other wellness brand |

## 3. Vocabulary & Language Guidelines

### Do Say:
| Category | Preferred Terms |
|---|---|
| **Brand references** | Ayurveda.Tech (always capitalised), the platform, your wellness journey |
| **Audience** | wellness seeker, health-conscious individual, practitioner, consultant, client |
| **Action** | begin your journey, book a consultation, discover your dosha, grow your practice |
| **Ayurvedic terms** | Vata, Pitta, Kapha (always capitalised), dosha, Prakriti, balance, Agni (digestive fire), Ojas (vitality) |
| **Benefits** | personalised care, expert-guided, root-cause healing, holistic wellness, timeless wisdom |
| **Technology** | digital consultations, online platform, video sessions, client portal, practitioner dashboard |

### Don't Say:
| Category | Avoid |
|---|---|
| **Clichés** | "New-age wellness," "healing vibes," "good energy" |
| **Clinical coldness** | "Patient intake," "medical history form," "treatment protocol" (prefer "health profile," "wellness plan") |
| **Sales pressure** | "Limited time offer," "act now," "don't miss out" |
| **Generic wellness** | "Mindfulness," "self-care," "wellness journey" (too generic — use specific Ayurveda language) |
| **Exclusionary terms** | "Alternative medicine" — we prefer "traditional medicine" or "ancient healing system" |
| **Over-promising** | "Cure," "guaranteed results," "fix your [condition]" — Ayurveda is about balance, not quick fixes |

### Dosha Personality References (For Content & Voice Modulations):
| Dosha | Tone Direction | Kind of Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Vata** | Grounding, calming, reassuring | Sleep routines, anxiety management, digestive health |
| **Pitta** | Cooling, clarifying, focused | Anti-inflammatory, skin health, stress reduction |
| **Kapha** | Energizing, motivating, encouraging | Weight management, seasonal detox, exercise guidance |

## 6. Content Principles

1. **Educate first, convert second** — every piece of content should provide value before asking for action
2. **Translate, don't dilute** — explain Ayurvedic concepts in accessible language without stripping their depth
3. **Dual-reader awareness** — content should work for someone who's never heard of doshas AND for a seasoned practitioner
4. **Show, don't tell** — use testimonials, case studies, and practitioner stories instead of claims
5. **Seasonal relevance** — align content with seasons (e.g., Kapha-balancing in spring, Vata-balancing in autumn)
6. **SEO with integrity** — target high-intent keywords naturally, never keyword-stuff
7. **Consistent terminology** — maintain a glossary of approved Ayurvedic terms and use them consistently across all content
8. **Practitioner voices** — feature practitioner-authored content to build credibility and platform community
