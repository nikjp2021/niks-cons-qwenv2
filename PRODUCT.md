# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Startup founders and SME owners (global, 15+ countries). Non-technical leaders exploring AI adoption. Existing businesses needing digital transformation. Companies seeking automation to reduce operational overhead.

Context: They're browsing on desktop/mobile, comparing consulting firms, looking for a trusted partner who can deliver AI-powered solutions. They need confidence that this firm understands their business challenges and can deliver measurable results.

## Product Purpose

Nik's Consulting is an AI-focused digital consulting and agency that helps startups and SMEs with:
- AI Solutions & Agentic AI
- AI Automation
- Digital Transformation
- Website & App Development
- AI-Powered Social Media
- Agile Project Management

Success looks like: Visitors convert to consultation bookings via "Get a Free Quote" CTA. Secondary: newsletter signup for lead nurturing.

## Brand Personality

**Bold** - confident, decisive, no-fluff
**Technical** - AI-native, data-driven, precise
**Approachable** - global SME focus, plain language, warm

Voice: Collaborative partner who works alongside clients. Confident but not arrogant. Precise but not jargon-heavy. Warm but professional.

3-word personality: Bold, Technical, Approachable

Emotional goals: Confidence, trust, excitement about possibilities, clarity about outcomes.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with purple-to-blue gradients
- Overly corporate consulting sites (McKinsey, BCG style)
- Flashy agency sites that prioritize style over substance
- Generic template sites with identical card grids
- Sites that use Inter/Roboto as primary display font
- Sites with heavy purple-to-blue gradients
- Sites with card-in-card-in-card nesting
- Sites with animation on every element

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach**: As an AI consulting firm, the site should demonstrate technical sophistication through clean code, smooth animations, and thoughtful UX, not just claims about AI expertise.

2. **Show, don't tell**: Use real case studies with measurable outcomes, counter animations for metrics, and interactive elements that demonstrate capability rather than just describing it.

3. **Expert confidence without arrogance**: Bold typography, strong visual hierarchy, and decisive design choices that convey expertise while remaining approachable to non-technical audiences.

4. **Global perspective, local relevance**: Design that works across cultures and contexts, with clear language and universal visual metaphors.

5. **Outcome-focused messaging**: Every section should connect to business results (ROI, growth, efficiency), not just features or capabilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance minimum
- Reduced motion support (prefers-reduced-motion)
- Semantic HTML with proper ARIA labels
- Keyboard navigation support
- Color contrast: minimum AA, target AAA for body text
- Alt text on all images
- Focus states visible on all focusable elements
